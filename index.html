Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to Ai Career Pilot. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our AI-powered resume analysis service, we collect the following information:

  • Account Data: When you sign in, we collect your email address and authentication details.
  • Resume and Job Description Data: We process the text extracted from the resume PDFs you upload and the job descriptions you paste into the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to run and improve the app features:

  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • To compare your resume against job descriptions and calculate keyword match scores.
  • To generate career analysis and feedback.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share specific data with trusted third-party services to perform essential app functions:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for secure user authentication and account management.
  • Gemini AI (Google): The resume text and job descriptions are sent to Google’s Gemini AI API solely to generate your analysis. We do not use your personal text to train public AI models.

4. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your resume text is processed in real-time for analysis. You can request the deletion of your account and account data at any time by contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
